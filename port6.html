AI-Powered WhatsApp Chatbot

An AI-powered WhatsApp chatbot designed to help customer service teams at companies and small businesses answer repetitive questions. Teams can add a knowledge base and use retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to support AI responses with relevant business information.

I worked as the Fullstack Developer and System Designer, covering the application interface, backend, and overall system architecture.

Key capabilities

  • AI-assisted responses supported by a configurable knowledge base and RAG.
  • A shared inbox for customer service teams, with manual replies and human handoff.
  • Rule-based message handling alongside AI responses.
  • A durable outbox and background workers for queued delivery and retries.
  • WhatsApp notifications for customer service handoffs.
