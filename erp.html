About the project

ERP Raho Premier is an internal system with interfaces for inventory and system management. The documentation shows product master data, units, conversions, and navigation for logistics and administrative workflows.

On the inventory master page, stock is scoped to a branch or Central Logistics. The interface includes SKUs, product categories, base UOM, usage UOM, conversion factors, and optional batch and expiry tracking.

My role

I contributed as a Fullstack Developer, working on the frontend and backend of ERP Raho Premier.

  • Developing the application interface for internal operations.
  • Developing the backend as part of the ERP implementation.

Interface scope

From product data to operations.

Modules and menus shown in the project documentation.

01 / PRODUCT DATA

Inventory master data

Products, categories, units of measure (UOM), conversion factors, default cost prices, and batch and expiry settings.

02 / STOCK

Stock & goods movements

Navigation covers stock, transfers, adjustments and stocktaking, item usage history, stock requests, and stock reservations.

03 / LOGISTICS

Shipping & receiving

Access to the Logistics Dashboard, shipments, goods receipt, Treatment BOM, shipment reports, and Homecare Bags.

04 / ADMINISTRATION

Purchasing & approval

Purchasing & AP, Deferred Revenue, and Approval Inbox are available in the operations navigation.

05 / SYSTEM MANAGEMENT

Permissions & audits

The interface provides access to Admin Managers, Product Master, Permission & Role, and Audit Log.

06 / WORKSPACE

Teams & integrations

Navigation includes RAIN · Task Assistant, Teams & Tasks, Voucher Partnership, Zoho Integration, and WhatsApp Settings.
